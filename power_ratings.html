NFL and NBA Power Ratings

I built two websites for calculating NFL and NBA team power ratings. Both use game results to estimate team strength, with adjustments for strength of schedule and home advantage. You can choose a season and a range of games, then compare teams in a matchup calculator.

The models use ordinary least squares regression. Ratings are centered at zero, so a positive rating means a team is above average over the selected games.

NFL Power Ratings

The NFL model uses scoring margin. A rating measures how many points a team is above or below an average team on a neutral field.

Choose a season and a start and end week to see the ratings, records, scoring margins, and strength of schedule for that period. The matchup calculator estimates a point margin for two teams, with a choice of home field or a neutral site. Weekly predictions show unplayed games using results from the weeks before the selected week.

NBA Power Ratings

The NBA model adjusts for pace. Its ratings measure points per 100 possessions above or below an average team, with adjustments for opponents and home court.

Choose a season, a date range, and regular-season games, playoffs, or all games. The table includes offensive and defensive efficiency, net rating, and strength of schedule. You can compare two teams on either home court or a neutral court.

The upcoming-games section shows model point spreads alongside sportsbook spreads supplied by ESPN. It converts the efficiency ratings into point margins using projected pace.
